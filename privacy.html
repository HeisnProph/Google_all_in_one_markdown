Privacy Policy for All in One markdown

Effective Date: September 10, 2026

Developer Contact: jingx.z223@gmail.com

1. Overview

All in One markdown ("we", "our", or "the App") is an online in-place Markdown editor that integrates with Google Drive. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website and in using our services.

2. Information We Collect and Access

The App requests access to your Google Account only to enable features requested by you, specifically accessing and saving Markdown files in your Google Drive.

  • Google User Data: When you connect your Google account, we request the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This only grants access to files that you explicitly create or open with this App.
  • No Storage of Credentials: OAuth tokens are held only in your browser's local memory/session during your active use and are never sent to or stored on our external servers.
  • File Content: The text and markdown documents you edit are processed in-place in your browser. We do not store, copy, or retain your private document contents on any remote server.

3. How We Use User Data

We use your data solely to provide the core functionalities of the App: reading markdown files you select, allowing you to edit them, and saving changes back to your Google Drive.

We do NOT:

  • Sell, rent, or lease your personal data or document content to any third parties.
  • Use your Google user data or document contents for advertising, marketing, or profiling.
  • Use your data to train, retrain, or improve generalized or personalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.

4. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

All in One markdown's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because document contents are rendered directly in your browser, no user documents are retained on our servers. You may revoke access to the App at any time via your Google Account Permissions page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: jingx.z223@gmail.com.
